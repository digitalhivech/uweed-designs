Default

The horizontal logo, dark blue on white or a light tint. White on dark, coloured or photographic backgrounds.

Colourways

Each colour is cleared for the backgrounds shown and no others. The three light versions sit on dark blue only. Black is for one-colour production where it is mandatory.

With tagline

For brand presentation with enough space. Never below 180px wide. All eight colourways exist; two are shown.

Mark

Social profile, favicon and tiny UI space. Six colours: there is no dark-green or dark-pink mark in the kit.

Secondary marks

The badge and the stamp support the logo, never replace it: a signature on explainer graphics, featured images, packaging, stickers and social posts. One per surface. Same eight colourways and backgrounds as the logo; four are shown. The brand line stays in English in every language.

Minimum sizes

Shown at the smallest permitted width. If the space is tighter, switch to the mark or drop the tagline rather than shrinking the logo.

Clear space

Keep at least 25% of the mark's height free on every side. The dashed frame is the outer edge of that space. The horizontal file already contains it, so never crop its canvas; the tagline and mark files are tighter, and the blue band is the margin to add in layout.

Never

  • Recolour an asset: pick the file in the colour you need
  • Rebuild the logo with a font, in Poppins or anything else
  • Add an outline, shadow, gradient or background box
  • Stretch it or change its proportions
  • Show it larger than its source when using a PNG
  • Use the leaf icon, or any icon, in place of the mark
  • Rotate a secondary mark, fill its rings or put anything inside them
  • Use a secondary mark instead of the logo, or two on one surface
