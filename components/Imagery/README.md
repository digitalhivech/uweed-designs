# Imagery

Category images and partner logos: canvas, framing and display (November 2026).

The page shows the two asset canvases with their framing guides, a row of category tiles and a row of partner logos of different shapes on a desktop, on a light-gray surface and on a 380px phone, what goes wrong when an image is cropped, stretched or padded, and a check of the supplied files against the guides.

- The rules live in the README, *Imagery*; the frame shapes in the tokens `category-frame-ratio` and `brand-logo-frame-ratio`. Change them there, not here.
- The visual treatment of the artwork is in the uWeed Imagery Master Guide, outside this system.
- Hero banner pictures have their rules in the README, *Imagery*, too. They are not shown on this page yet.
- The examples are real files, stored as assets in the *Imagery* group: four category images (CBD Blüten, Hash, Joints, Kristalle & Isolat) and three partner logos (B-Chill, Bud Bouncy, Naturalpes). They are specimens for the rules, not the live catalogue: the shop's images stay in WordPress.
- The fourth logo tile is a grey stand-in for an opaque white-background file. No such file was supplied; it shows the case to avoid.
- Measured November 2026: Hash and the three logos are on target. Blüten is slightly tight at the top (7.2%), Joints leaves more room at the top (11.9%), and Kristalle & Isolat sits low (base at 93.9%, 6.1% clear at the bottom) and should be re-exported higher.
