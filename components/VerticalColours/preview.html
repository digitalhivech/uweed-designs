Vape's dark tone is the navy itself. In the vape vertical the dark tone and the shared engraving colour are one value, so its imagery leans on medium-blue and light-blue. Unlike the other medium tones, medium-blue takes white (7.20:1).

Orange next to brown

The two warm families share a hue; saturation and lightness keep them apart. Colour difference measured as CIEDE2000: around 2 is barely visible, above 10 is a clearly different colour at a glance.

dark-orange to dark-brown is 25.6. The light tints are the closest pair: never let them be the only thing that tells the snus and tobacco verticals apart.

With navy, white and the neutrals

Flat stand-ins for category imagery: the light tone as ground, the medium tone as the subject, the navy engraving on top, the dark tone as a packaging band with white text. The tiles sit on white with the hairline, on a light-gray block, with dark-gray body copy.

The engraving stays navy wherever it holds 3:1 (it is a graphic, not text): on every light tone and on medium green, pink, orange and brown. On medium-blue (2.60:1) it turns white, as in the vape tile, and also on dark-green (2.82:1) and dark-brown (1.52:1). The category names and copy here are placeholders.
