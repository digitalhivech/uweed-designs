# VerticalColours

Five shop verticals, one colour family each, for category imagery and creative assets (proposed, November 2026).

The page shows the five families side by side (dark, medium and light tone with token and hex), the orange and brown families next to each other with their measured colour difference, each family with the shared navy, white and the neutrals, and the contrast of every proposed pairing.

- The values live in the tokens; change them there, not here.
- The rules are in the README, *Vertical colours: one family per shop*. The interface colour roles do not change: a vertical colour is never a button, link, badge, message or state.
- `dark-orange`, `dark-brown`, `medium-brown` and `light-brown` are a first proposal, to be refined visually.
- The flat shapes on this page stand in for category imagery. No imagery exists in the system yet.
