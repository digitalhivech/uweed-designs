A library re-implements in JavaScript what the browser now does natively, on the main thread. Native pinch and scrolling are composited, so they stay smooth on the mid-range Android that matters most for mobile-heavy traffic.

The performance decision that actually matters is not the library. Full-resolution images load on open, never on page load — the gallery keeps its srcset at display size, the lightbox holds data-full, and src is assigned when the dialog opens. Most visitors never open it and never pay for it.

Two caveats: iOS pinch inside a fixed dialog is historically unreliable, so double-tap is the mechanism and pinch the enhancement. And <dialog> needs Safari 15.4+.

Six of the seven share one root cause: FiboSearch renders the DOM and offers no hook, so there is nothing to style. The states above are the target, not the current build.

The per-instance limit is the structural one. Banner search and header search are the same panel with the same settings — not a styling gap but a ceiling on what can ever be specified separately.
